# FUTURE_UIUX_02
For this task, I designed a mobile appointment booking app for the same salon, Glamazon. The main objective was to make the booking process simple, quick and easy for users, especially for first-time users. 

I designed this app step by step, starting from service selection to final booking confirmation. Each and every screen has a clear purpose, so the users don’t feel confused or overwhelmed while navigating through the app. 

The service selection screen allows users to quickly choose what they need without too many options. After that, the date and time selection screen is designed in a way that makes available slots easy to view and select. 

Before confirming the booking, users can see a summary of their selected service, date, and time, which will build a sense of trust among the users, reduce mistakes and give users confidence in their booking. A final confirmation screen is added to provide clear feedback that the appointment has been successfully booked. 

While designing, I focused on keeping everything touch-friendly, clean, and easy to read on a mobile screen. The overall goal was to reduce effort for users and create a smooth and reliable booking experience.
